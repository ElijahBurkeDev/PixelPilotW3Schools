PIXEL PILOT

HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to Pixel Pilot! Weave through obstacles using UP/DOWN or W/S keys. Pressing ESC or switching tabs will pause the game. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick and FIRE button. The further you go, the more Pixel Pieces you earn — spend them in the hangar on new ships! Press SPACE to shoot orange barriers out of the way. Good luck, pilot!
